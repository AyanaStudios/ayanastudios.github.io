Core Learning Suite

Comprehensive early curriculum covering Alphabets, Numbers 1–20, Letter Tracing, Handwriting practice, and Crayon Coloring activities.

🍓

Learn & Discover Worlds

Explore everyday concepts through friendly clay-style characters: fresh Fruits, Vegetables, primary & secondary Colors, 2D Shapes, and Vehicles.

🎯

Interactive Color & Vehicle Sorting

Drag-and-drop gameplay teaching categorization: sort fruits by color (Red vs Yellow buckets) and sort vehicles by where they travel (Road vs Sky).

🧩

Play & Practice Activity Center

Fun-filled mini-games including Before & After math sequences, Pop the Color balloon tap, Match It pairs, Memory cards, and Shape puzzles.
